## Cross-source connections and tensions

Author's own cross-references are [S]; the rest [I].

1. [S] Self-positioning: Leads sits on Offers (file 007); MM Ten Years (file 040) frames Offers = what to sell, Leads = how to find people, MM = how to get them to buy. MM cites the Offers bonuses chapter (file 016), the Leads affiliates chapter (file 013) and Leads for advertising (file 039); T-Sales cites the Leads employees chapter (§42); T-Affiliates reads the Leads definition (§1); T-OffersLM cites MM (§4).
2. **3:1 across four framings.** Leads: LTGP:CAC observed pattern (file 020), cold-outreach minimum (file 018), employees "one-third of lifetime profit" (file 024), affiliates floor (file 026); consistent. T-CaseSaaS §13 adds the dynamic reading (10:1 sliding to 3:1 in a year = reputation). Offers only defines LTGP [p38].
3. **Delay the ask vs 30-day cash.** Offers [p128] and T-Content ~47:00-52:00 vs MM Section I/VI and Leads file 020. Sources' own reconciliation: T-Content is an audience asset funded by other businesses; MM addresses a bootstrapped operator who otherwise "runs out of cash." T-Sales §45 uses "bigger plane, longer runway" for pre-education, not monetization timing.
4. **"Advertising lets a terrible product make money" vs referrals need quality.** Leads file 007 vs file 023 and Roadmap Level 4 (file 031). Stage-dependent: advertising buys chances early; word of mouth needs a product that delights.
5. **Give until they ask vs Rule-of-100 asks.** Leads file 016 vs the same chapter's ask scripts and files 021, 029. Resolution in the source: give in public, ask in private; integrated vs intermittent by platform; unsure → lead magnet.
6. **Lower promises vs Grand Slam dream outcome.** Leads file 023 way 2 and T-CaseSaaS §9 vs Offers [p76, p181]. Offers itself calls big claims "the easiest to establish" and shifts to the bottom of the equation [p76]; T-LeadMagnets §7 adds "specific on negatives, vaguer on positives."
7. **Network-effect warning for 1:1 services vs service advice elsewhere.** T-Moats #1: a traditional service business deteriorates as customers are added. Offers, Leads and MM assume services scale (DFY → DWY [p106-107]; one-to-many assets [p113]; MM feature ladder files 030-031). The author's answer across sources is productizing or one-to-many delivery.
8. **Price-to-wallet ethics vs "never the same thing for less."** T-Moats #7 permits price discrimination only by tweaking the offer; MM file 026 and T-Sales §51 forbid the same thing for less. Consistent once "tweak" = feature change; MM Feature Downsells and Payment Plans are the sanctioned tools.
9. **Guarantees as conversion lever vs anti-guarantee / control.** Offers Ch15 ("number one way to increase conversion", [p156]) vs T-Sales §39 (he "doesn't guarantee most of what he sells"; control rule) and MM's uses as paid upsell (file 020), removable feature (files 030-031) and stripped from decoys (file 014). Offers already conditions the choice by ticket and fulfilment cost [p157-158, p162]. Same tool, different positions in the sequence.
10. **Direct-to-offer ads vs lead-magnet ads over the horizon.** T-OffersLM §5: direct ads win up-front ROAS; the lead magnet wins overall ROAS and scales. Leads file 012: "try the core offer first", lead magnet for "more expensive stuff"; T-LeadMagnets §6 and T-OffersLM §9: no lead magnet = fewer leads. Varies by revenue target ("smaller amount" vs "huge realms").
11. **CFA is the seed of Money Models.** Leads file 020 states it as a paid-ads cash-flow fix with an immediate upsell; MM expands it into four offer types. The MM notes' open question is resolved: Leads' 2x is the per-ad test budget, not a CFA threshold; MM's $100M standard is "at least two more customers."
12. **Premium pricing vs price-low-then-raise.** Offers [p69] vs MM file 039 and Leads free-first-five (file 014). Stage distinction: low while learning, high once reliable; Offers' "create flow, then add friction" [p106] agrees.
13. **Referrals as a system vs referrals inside offers.** Leads file 023 vs MM (WYMB criteria, two-prize giveaway, gift card, barter downsell) and T-Affiliates §7 ("promoters"). Offers only has partner IOUs [p151-152].
14. **Continuity and retention.** Offers barely covers it; MM supplies the mechanics (file 036, 027); T-CaseSaaS supplies CS operations. Proactive downgrade (T-CaseSaaS §16 t8) and feature-downselling current customers (MM files 030-031) are the same practice; both call them "second-highest value."
15. **Transparency vs anchoring (within MM).** "Be transparent" (file 009) vs giveaway prospects not knowing the price until the call (file 013) and seesaw framing (file 027). Reconciliation: anchors must be real prices actually sold (files 034-035).
16. **Discount thresholds vary by mechanism.** 10-30% of gross margin (MM Giveaways); ≤25% (MM Rollover); 20-50% (MM Pay Less Now); 10-20% "pointless", 80-90% works on a cheap splinter component (T-OffersLM §8); 90% gift cards (Leads file 023). Keep the object of each discount distinct.
17. **"Hard selling is for weak products" (MM file 009) vs "he who asks the most times gets the most sales" (T-Sales §31).** T-Sales' own limits: stop when the prospect is better served not buying (§39-40); rapport permits repeated asks.
18. **Once monetized, rarely change the offer (Offers [p179-180]) vs one test per week (Leads file 021).** Compatible via Offers' variation order: test wrappers, keep the monetization structure.
19. **Follow-up cadence variations.** Warm: 3 touches over 3 days (Leads file 014). Cold: more times, more ways, email first, restart after 3-6 months (file 018).
